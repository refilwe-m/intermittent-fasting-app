# Fasting App

I am on a weight loss journey and I am trying to have a central place that can act as journal later on of how I achieved my goal. It should include basic fasting tracker, if I am doing OMAD, I should just check-in when I eat, if I am on 16/8 should tell me when I am on fasting mode or eating mode.

## Features

- Daily Streak checker like the github contributions tracker. Should be color coded. Using these checkers
  - I went to the gym
  - I fasted (Any type)
  - I ran
  - I walked and reached my steps goal
  - I hiked
  - Maybe we can add other for any extra thing I did that day, that would count as an activity towards weight loss
- Track my fasting if I am on Fast or Eating based on the chosen fasting type for the day, and the state I am in based on the hours fasted.
- Track my water intake
- Ask me to record my weight every 3 weeks
- Must be a PWA
- Must have AI Suggestions for me on what to try or commit more on based on the progress I am making, free open source ai
- Have a goal tracker dashboard, showing current weight, BMI, streaks fasted hours and thing that you think may help.

## Value

I want something that would help me also keep a habit of just doing something daily and check, and in a way help me with commitment and to be mindful of my decisions and keep me accountable.
